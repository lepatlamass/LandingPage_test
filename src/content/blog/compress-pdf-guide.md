## Introduction

Working with large PDF files can be a headache, especially when you need to email them or upload them to portals with strict size limits. Fortunately, compressing PDFs is easier than ever with Refinedocs.

## Why Compress PDFs?

1. **Faster Uploads:** Smaller files upload in seconds rather than minutes.
2. **Email Compatibility:** Most email clients restrict attachments to 25MB or less.
3. **Storage Efficiency:** Save space on your hard drive or in the cloud.
