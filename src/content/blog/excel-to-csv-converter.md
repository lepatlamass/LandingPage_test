## 🧰 Pro Tips for Flawless Conversions

Get the most out of your Excel to CSV workflow:

🔹 **Clean your data first**: Remove blank rows/columns to avoid empty CSV fields.  
🔹 **Use consistent headers**: First row = column names for easy importing.  
🔹 **Avoid merged cells**: They don't translate well to CSV's flat structure.  
🔹 **Save dates in ISO format** (`YYYY-MM-DD`) for maximum compatibility.  
🔹 **Test with a small file first** when working with critical data.
