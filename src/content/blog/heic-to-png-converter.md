## 🔍 What Is HEIC (And Why Does It Cause Problems)?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Format) is Apple's modern photo format. It's great for saving space on your iPhone — but not so great for sharing.

**The challenge**:  
❌ Many Windows PCs can't open HEIC files without extra codecs  
❌ Most websites, email clients, and design tools prefer PNG or JPG  
❌ Android devices often struggle with HEIC compatibility  
❌ Professional software may require conversion before editing

**The solution**: Convert HEIC to PNG — a universally supported, lossless format that works everywhere.
