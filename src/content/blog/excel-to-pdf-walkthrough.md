## Sharing Data Securely

Excel is unmatched for data analysis, but it's not always the best format for sharing. Formulas can be accidentally overwritten, and formatting can get messed up depending on the viewer's software.

Converting your Excel files to PDF ensures that your data remains locked, secure, and prints exactly as intended.

## How to Convert

1. Finish all calculations and formatting in Excel.
2. Navigate to our converter dashboard.
3. Upload the `.xlsx` or `.csv` file.
4. Download the generated PDF. 

It takes less than a minute, and ensures a professional presentation for your clients or managers.
