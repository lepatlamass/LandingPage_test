## 🛡️ Why Watermark Your Files?

Before diving into the tool, let's talk about why watermarking matters:
* **Theft Deterrence:** A visible watermark makes it incredibly difficult for someone to pass off your work as their own.
* **Brand Awareness:** Adding your logo to images ensures that as your content is shared across the web, your brand name travels with it.
* **Document Security:** Marking business documents as "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT" prevents unauthorized distribution and miscommunication.
