## 🔍 What Is OCR (And Why Should You Care)?

OCR stands for **Optical Character Recognition** — technology that "reads" text inside images and converts it into digital, editable content.

**Why it matters**:  
✅ Save hours of manual typing  
✅ Digitize handwritten notes, receipts, or documents  
✅ Make scanned PDFs searchable and editable  
✅ Extract quotes, references, or data from screenshots  
✅ Accessibility: Convert image-based text for screen readers

In short: **OCR turns pictures into productivity.**

## 💡 Pro Tips for Flawless Text Extraction

Get the most out of every conversion:

🔹 **Use clear, well-lit images**: Sharp photos with good contrast yield the best OCR results  
🔹 **Keep text horizontal**: Tilted or rotated text may reduce accuracy  
🔹 **Crop to the text area**: Remove unnecessary background to help the engine focus  
🔹 **Download before you refresh**: Your extracted text is ready instantly — copy or save it right away  
🔹 **Proofread important extractions**: While our accuracy is high, complex fonts or handwriting may need a quick review
