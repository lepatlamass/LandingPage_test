## 🔍 Why Convert Image Formats? (And Why It Matters)

Different formats serve different purposes. Knowing when to convert can save you time, bandwidth, and frustration.

**Convert to JPG when**:  
✅ You need smaller file sizes for emails, social media, or web pages  
✅ Compatibility is key — JPG works everywhere  

**Convert to PNG when**:  
✅ You need transparency (like logos or overlays)  
✅ You want lossless quality for graphics, screenshots, or designs  

**Convert to WEBP when**:  
✅ You're optimizing for modern web performance  
✅ You want great quality with even smaller files than JPG  

**Convert to GIF when**:  
✅ You need simple animations or lightweight motion graphics  
✅ You're sharing memes, tutorials, or quick demos  

**Convert to BMP or other formats when**:  
✅ Legacy software or specific workflows require it  
✅ You need uncompressed quality for editing  

In short: **The right format = better results, faster.**
