## 1. Usage Limits and Paywalls

The biggest differentiator between Refinedocs and Smallpdf is how they restrict free users.

- **Smallpdf:** The free tier of Smallpdf restricts you to **2 tasks per day**. If you convert a PDF to Word and then realize you also need to compress it, you will hit a paywall and must wait 24 hours or sign up for a 7-day free trial.
- **Refinedocs:** Refinedocs offers **unlimited conversions**. You can process 5, 20, or 100 documents back-to-back without hitting a paywall or encountering a popup requesting a credit card.

---

## 2. Privacy & Processing Architecture

How each platform handles your sensitive documents is a major security consideration.

- **Smallpdf (Cloud-Based):** When you upload a file to Smallpdf, it is sent to their remote servers, processed in the cloud, and stored on their servers for up to an hour before being deleted. For corporate financials, medical records, or sensitive contracts, this represents an external data transfer.
- **Refinedocs (Client-Side Local):** Refinedocs is built using modern WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. This means your files **never leave your device**. The conversion, compression, or extraction happens locally inside your browser. No remote servers ever see your content, providing maximum compliance and security.
