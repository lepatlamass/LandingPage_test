## 💡 Lossy vs. Lossless: The Secret to "0 Visibility Loss"

How can you shrink a file size by 80% without making it look pixelated or blurry? 

The answer lies in **perceptual quality compression**. 

High-resolution PNGs are **lossless**. They store every single color value for every pixel. This is great for printing large posters, but completely unnecessary for computer screens, tablets, or phones.

JPEGs use **lossy compression**. This format uses mathematical algorithms to discard visual data that the human eye is physically incapable of perceiving (such as tiny variations in color gradients within shadows or sky). 

By converting a high-res PNG to JPEG at a high-quality setting (such as **80%**), you remove megabytes of invisible data while leaving the visual image completely pristine. To your audience, the before and after look 100% identical.
